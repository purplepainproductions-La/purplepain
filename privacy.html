Privacy Policy

Prince G's Bayou Boogie Beatdown · Last updated 17 August 2026

This policy covers the Android game Prince G's Bayou Boogie Beatdown (package com.sspurplepain.bayouboogie), published by SS.Purplepain Studio / Purple Pain Productions.

The short version

The game collects nothing, sends nothing, and requests no permissions. It does not ask for the internet, storage, location, camera, microphone, contacts, or your advertising ID. It has no account system, no analytics, no crash reporting, and no third-party SDKs.

Data we collect

None. No personal information, no device identifiers, no usage or diagnostic data leaves your device, because the app has no network permission and therefore no way to send any.

Data we share

None. Nothing is collected, so there is nothing to share, sell, or disclose to anyone.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your own progress locally — unlocked stages, currency, perks and settings. This file lives in the app's private storage on your device and is never transmitted. Uninstalling the app deletes it. We cannot see it and cannot recover it.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone, regardless of age.

Distribution platforms

Google Play and itch.io are separate companies with their own privacy policies. When you download or purchase the game, that transaction happens with them, not with us, and is governed by their terms. We receive no personal information from either beyond aggregate sales figures.

Changes to this policy

Advertising is planned for a future update. When rewarded ads ship, the app will require the INTERNET permission and Google's advertising SDK will process an advertising identifier in order to serve them, with a consent prompt where the law requires one. This page and the app's Google Play Data Safety declaration will both be updated before that version is released, and this section will name the date it changed. The version described above is what ships today.
